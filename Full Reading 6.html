But unlike Fiordland to the south, which was and still is an uninhabitable wilderness, the Coast in the 1930s was not only habitable, it was also potentially rich...

Maurice Buckley, a World War I pilot, was the first to give Coasters such wings, by establishing the Arrow Aviation Company in 1923...

Next came an aviator named Bert Mercer, who made a reconnaissance flight to the Coast in August 1933 and started Air Travel (NZ)...
